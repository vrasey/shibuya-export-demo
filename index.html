The Export Process

A transparent, step-by-step breakdown of how we secure and ship your vehicle.

1. Select your vehicle

Choose a car from a Japanese dealer listing. We contact the dealer to confirm availability, verify the condition and history, and check the price against current market value.

2. Invoice and full payment

Once you confirm the vehicle, we issue an invoice for the full amount upfront. Payment goes directly to the bank account of our partner shipping and export company, keeping the transaction smooth and secure.

3. We secure the vehicle

After your transfer is confirmed, we complete the purchase with the dealer and secure the car in your name. If the vehicle is sold before we can secure it, your funds are returned in full to the account they came from.

4. Purchase confirmed

With funds already in place, we move straight into export: documentation, inland transport, and shipping arrangements.

5. Your vehicle ships

Once the car is loaded, we send you the shipping details, vessel information, and estimated arrival at your destination port.
